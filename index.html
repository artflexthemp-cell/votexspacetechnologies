Our Main Project

Compassion.Sat 1

Compassion.Sat 1 is our Earth-based, satellite-inspired environmental monitoring project. It explores how electronic sensors and an ESP32 development board can collect local environmental measurements.

Project Objectives

  • Measure local temperature with a suitable sensor.
  • Measure humidity with a compatible sensor.
  • Collect readings through an ESP32.
  • Display measurements on a web dashboard.
  • Learn how environmental data can be understood.

Compassion.Sat 1 operates on Earth. It is not an orbital satellite and is not designed to launch into space. Its capabilities will be developed and tested as the hardware and software progress.
